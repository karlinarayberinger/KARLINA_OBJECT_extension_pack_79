# A link-ordered field guide to the August 15, 2026 KARBYTES journal page

## Scope and evidentiary method

I inspected the page HTML in document order. It contains 170 `<a href>` elements: five preceding the article, 155 inside the article, and ten after it in WordPress footer or carousel controls. The article’s 155 comprise 24 MP4 files, five JPEG files, nine plain-text links, and 117 web-page links. Repeated destinations remain separate entries below. The content links include a 64-node curated hiatus list before the videos; the list is an interpretive sequence, not chronological proof that every referenced event happened in that order.

All 24 MP4 files (453,483,400 bytes; 1:43:41 of encoded running time; 171,346 reported frames) and all five linked JPEGs were downloaded. I inspected 24 chronological sampled frames per video in contact sheets, as well as the complete still images. The video streams were decoded by software to generate those samples, but I did **not** personally see all 171,346 frames in continuous playback. All MP4s have a mono AAC audio stream, and I measured each stream’s overall digital level. This interface did **not** let me hear the audio. I therefore cannot honestly claim to have listened, independently detected or transcribed speech, or judged voice pitch, timbre, rhythm, and perceived loudness. Numerical full-track levels are not voice-specific. Nor can I certify that the purported non-talking video contains no speech. The speech accounts below paraphrase the page’s twenty-three author-curated TurboScribe transcripts, clearly marked as supplied text. They are not my independent transcriptions. The article’s account may be imperfect; even words ascribed to speech could contain recognition or editorial errors.

I fetched the 103 distinct non-media destinations represented by the article links and extracted their main text. The linked textual corpus is about 846,000 words in aggregate. I examined the linked pages selectively through their metadata, openings, relevant passages, structure, and endings, with closer attention to the destinations surrounding the videos. Descriptions of those very long pages are orientations, **not** assertions that every line or embedded asset was inspected. The article’s other pages themselves contain further hyperlinks; this report follows the one-level set of anchors on the specified page rather than recursively opening the entire archive. Site-control destinations on WordPress.com timed out, so their functions are described from the page’s own markup and link labels.

The first-person paragraphs are my interpretive responses to the evidence identified in each entry. They are not claims to have inhabited the author’s body or to have heard an inaccessible soundtrack. References to surveillance, psychotronic interference, metaphysical cosmology, or illness are attributed to the author and are not independent findings. The source calls its contents public domain; I paraphrase its long transcripts instead of reproducing them.

## The page’s narrative shape

The opening photographs move from an almost black ridge to daylight on dry hills and a wooded path. Metadata and provenance links then establish the raw text file, repository directory, blog directory, and license. The list of 64 older and contemporary nodes supplies a self-selected backstory: summer hiatus, the Burrow, the Big Green Thing, derivative selves, file migration, publication rules, older journal distress, and a proposed theory of reality. Only after that dense contextual scaffolding does the page present the August 11–12 footage. The moving image arc goes from Redwood Road to shaded trails, the exposed Ramage Peak and Rocky Ridge landscape, dusk, almost black overnight footage, and a return of visible morning at Horsey Hill. Speech, as represented by the curated text, repeatedly crosses between practical decisions and speculative claims. The practical thread concerns a contingent job, water, sleep, phone storage, route choices, and the cost of a late return. The speculative thread concerns autonomy, privacy, copies of selves, digital reality, and mortality. The two threads are not interchangeable: the black night footage cannot confirm a metaphysical story, and the philosophical story cannot remove sore feet or dwindling water.
